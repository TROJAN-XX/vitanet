## How to File a Grievance

1. **In-app reporting**: Use the Report button on content or profiles
2. **Email**: Send details to the Grievance Officer email
3. **Include**:
   - Your registered username/email
   - Description of the issue
   - Relevant screenshots or links
   - Date and time of the incident

## Response Timeline

- **Acknowledgement**: Within 24 hours of receiving the complaint
- **Resolution**: Within 15 days of receiving the complaint
- **Complex cases**: If additional time is needed, we will inform you of the expected timeline

## Types of Grievances

- Content that violates Community Guidelines
- Account suspension or ban appeals
- Privacy concerns or data requests
- Intellectual property complaints
- Any other platform-related complaints

## Resolution Process

1. Grievance received and logged
2. Acknowledgement sent to complainant
3. Investigation and review
4. Action taken (if applicable)
5. Resolution communicated to complainant
6. All actions logged in audit system
