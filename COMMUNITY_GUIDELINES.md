# Community Guidelines — VitaNet

VitaNet is an independent creator network. These guidelines exist to keep the community safe, respectful, and creative.

## Our Principles

1. **Creators own their work** — Respect intellectual property and give credit
2. **Be respectful** — Treat others as you'd want to be treated
3. **No engagement bait** — Authentic content over viral manipulation
4. **Transparency** — We explain how content is ranked and moderated

## What's Not Allowed

### Harassment & Bullying
- Targeted harassment, threats, or intimidation
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- Coordinated attacks against individuals

### Hate Speech
- Content promoting hatred based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics

### Sexual Content
- Sexually explicit content
- Non-consensual intimate images

### Violence & Dangerous Content
- Graphic violence or gore
- Content promoting self-harm or suicide
- Content promoting dangerous activities

### Spam & Manipulation
- Fake accounts or impersonation
- Automated posting or engagement manipulation
- Misleading content designed solely for engagement

### Illegal Activity
- Content promoting illegal activities
- Copyright infringement
- Privacy violations

### Impersonation
- Pretending to be another person or organization
- Using someone else's identity or likeness without permission

## Reporting

If you see content that violates these guidelines:
1. Use the **Report** button on any post, comment, or profile
2. Select the reason that best describes the violation
3. Add details if helpful
4. Our moderation team will review the report

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
- **Warning**: First-time minor violations
- **Content removal**: Content that violates guidelines is removed
- **Account suspension**: Temporary suspension for repeated violations
- **Account ban**: Permanent ban for severe or repeated violations

All moderation actions are logged in our audit system.

## Appeals

If you believe a moderation action was made in error, contact us through the grievance process outlined in our [Grievance Policy](GRIEVANCE.md).
