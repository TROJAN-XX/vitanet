# Privacy Policy — VitaNet

*Last updated: September 2026*

> **Disclaimer**: This document is prepared for engineering legal readiness. It is NOT a substitute for legal advice from a qualified attorney. Obtain professional legal review before public or commercial launch.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Account information**: Username, email address, display name, bio
- **Content**: Photos, videos, captions, comments you create and share
- **Communications**: Reports, support requests

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage data**: Pages visited, features used, timestamps
- **Device information**: Browser type, operating system (from User-Agent)
- **Security data**: Hashed IP addresses for rate limiting and abuse prevention

### 1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- Precise geolocation
- Contact lists or phone numbers
- Financial information
- Date of birth (we use age confirmation only)
- Third-party tracking pixels or advertising identifiers

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain the VitaNet service
- To verify your identity and email
- To enable social features (following, likes, comments)
- To enforce community guidelines and moderate content
- To prevent abuse and ensure security
- To send transactional emails (verification, password reset)

## 3. Data Storage

- **Metadata** (usernames, captions, relationships): Stored in MongoDB Atlas
- **Media** (photos, videos): Stored in Cloudflare R2
- **No data is sold to third parties**
- **No advertising data is collected**

## 4. Your Rights

- **Access**: View your data through your profile and settings
- **Export**: Download your data via Settings > Export Data
- **Deletion**: Delete your account and data via Settings > Delete Account
- **Correction**: Update your profile information at any time
- **Block/Mute**: Control who can interact with you

## 5. Data Retention

- Active accounts: Data retained while account is active
- Deleted accounts: Data removed within 30 days of deletion request
- Security logs: Retained for up to 90 days
- Audit events: Retained for up to 1 year

## 6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure authentication, and access controls. See our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) for details.

## 7. Children's Privacy

VitaNet is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of material changes via email or in-app notification.

## 9. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact the project maintainer through the repository.

---

*This privacy policy is prepared with reference to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and general data protection principles. Professional legal review is required before commercial deployment.*
